How Trading Works in Plain English

If you have never traded before, this 8-card visual guide gives you instant mastery of the core concepts — zero confusing math.

🟢
CALL (CE) = Betting UP

Buying a CALL means you profit when the stock market goes UP. If Nifty climbs +50 pts, your Call gains value.

🔴
PUT (PE) = Betting DOWN

Buying a PUT means you profit when the stock market falls. You can make money even when the market crashes!

🛡️
Stop Loss = Safety Seatbelt

An automated safety limit that sells your position if the price moves wrong, guaranteeing you never lose more than a set budget (e.g. ₹300).

🎯
Target = Cash Out Goal

Your predetermined profit exit (e.g. ₹600). We aim for 1:2 R:R (risk ₹1 to make ₹2), so winning 50% of trades makes you profitable!

📦
1 Lot = Minimum Pack

Options trade in bundles (e.g. 65 shares in Nifty). Your total investment is simply Price × 65 (e.g. ₹38 × 65 = ₹2,470).

⚡
2-Loss Rule = Account Shield

If 2 trades hit their stop loss in a single day, the platform locks new trades. This saves you from emotional revenge trading!

⏳
Time Decay (Theta) = Melting Ice Cube

Options expire. If Nifty sits flat for 1 hour, your option price drops even if the market doesn't fall. Avoid trading in flat midday chop!

🧠
1-Lot Discipline = Emotion Free

Always stick to 1 lot while learning. 1 lot keeps your risk tiny (₹300), eliminates panic, and builds real consistency.
